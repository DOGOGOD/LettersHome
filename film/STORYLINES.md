# 家书 · Storylines

## A — 灯下 (recommended, built)
Device: five real letters, five lamps. Form: match cuts on the flame — every chapter opens on the light a letter was written by, and ends when that light goes out.
The viewer meets a writer under a lamp, hears the letter in the writer's own language, sees the person it was written to, then learns the writer never came home. The epilogue turns the lamps around: the lights left burning at home, waiting.
First two seconds: black, a single ember drifts up; Du Fu's line 「烽火连三月，家书抵万金」 burns in as brush ink.
Beats: 757 prologue → 1861 Ballou → 1911 林觉民 → 1914 Floch (+ the enemy trench, the Christmas truce) → 1936 赵一曼 → 1942 左权 → letters arrive → field of graves → "我很好，勿念" today → title.
Look: 2.39:1 scope, film stills and generated footage graded per era; Source Han Serif subtitles, brush calligraphy titles.
Sound: original-language voices; solo cello + piano elegy; each era's real ambience (rain, fire, train, wind); "Stille Nacht" hummed in the truce.

## B — 邮差 (not built)
A timeless postman walks through 1,200 years of wars carrying one mailbag; each letter he delivers opens a chapter. One continuous take that walks from world to world.

## C — 展柜 (not built)
Present day: a girl in a museum reads letters in glass cases; each one dissolves her into the night it was written. Ends with her texting her own mother.
